About me

I’m a fifth-year PhD student at the Operations Research Center at MIT, advised by Prof. Rahul Mazumder.

My research focuses on optimization methods for efficient and reliable machine learning. I work on model compression, including pruning and quantization of LLMs, VLMs, and recommendation models. I also study interpretable additive models and develop new algorithms for detecting and correcting anomalies in large-scale datasets.

I was a Student Researcher at Google Research in 2024 and YouTube in 2026. Before my PhD, I completed the Master of Business Analytics at MIT and M.S. in Engineering, Computer Science at CentraleSupélec.
